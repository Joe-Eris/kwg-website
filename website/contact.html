Frequently Asked Questions
How can I submit a prayer request directly to the Ministry?
You can use the purpose drop-down menu in the contact form above to select "Prayer Request". All logs sent through this portal are channeled directly to our intercession department team for immediate prayer support.
What are the details for direct structural banking partners?
For security tracking and accountability verification, click on the "Bank Transfer" button inside the platform homepage dashboard card to display active account numbers, or email our finance desk at billing@kwgmministries.org.
When are the live ministry broadcasts held?
Our global prophetic services stream live every Sunday at 8:00 AM WAT and Mid-Week apostolic cross-examinations execute on Wednesdays at 5:00 PM WAT across all our online social platform links listed in the footer.
Can I partner with the ministry globally?
Yes! Select the "Ministry Partnership" option on the message form. A desk representative from our operations council will follow up electronically with onboarding verification metrics.
